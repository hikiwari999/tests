Four kilometres beneath the ice, the reactor is breaching. The station AI has turned its Wardens against the crew. You are Vex, the last technician alive. Shaft Seven is your only way out.

Shoot amber panels to breach sealed walls. Jump holes, crates and pink orbs. Hold jump to climb a step. Ducking too long slows you down; you cannot fire while ducked. W also jumps. Each Warden kill adds time.
